How The Season Is Laid Out
Two seasons. Ten weeks each.
One practice per week. The spring season builds directly on the fall. Practices 1–3 grow mindset, 4–6 build healthy habits, 7–9 strengthen confidence.
Each practice objective is grounded in Bloom's Taxonomy, getting more complex as the season goes on.

One Practice · Three Session Formats
Know your format before week one.
Two × 60
Split The Practice
Session A: coach message, activity, intentions, boxing intro. Session B: journal, training, burnout, shout-outs. The journal and the assessment stay in the session they are printed in.
One × 90
As Written, Unhurried
Run every block in order at the printed times. Spend the extra 30 minutes on mitt work and individual feedback.
One × 60
As Written, Tight
Coach message 15 · intentions 5 · training 30 · burnout 7 · shout-outs 3. Cut the review drill first. Keep journal prompts short or send them home.
Practice 01 runs long on the coach message and short on training. Plan for it.

When time is short, cut the review drill first.

Site Visits · Why We Come
Whether this works matters to us.
So we are moving to a model built on training, data collection and evaluation — assessments and surveys — rather than oversight. The proof comes from what you record, not from someone standing over your shoulder.
We will still visit every site a minimum of twice a school year. Not to catch you out: to see what is working, find where you need support, and make sure every girl at every site gets the same program.
After each visit we debrief with you, and you get a score and notes within two days.
How Site Visits Work
Twice a year, minimum. Thirty points.
Scores are diagnostic, not punitive.
Visit One · Weeks 1–5
Sets a baseline and sees how you have set things up. Fall: around Oct 12. Spring: around Feb 22.
Visit Two · Weeks 6–10
Celebrates what has grown and helps with anything still rough. Fall: Nov 2–16. Spring: Mar 15–Apr 5.

If you suspect a girl is being hurt, you report.
Step One

You Are Protected

Boundaries · Child Protection Policy
Never one-on-one. Never private.
The Two-Adult Rule
Box United prohibits one-on-one situations that are not observable by another adult.
Approved Channels Only
Keep communication group-based. Do not message participants privately or connect on personal social media.
Asked Most Often
Sessions, girls, and time.
I have to miss a session, or the school cancels one.
Notify Box United as soon as you know and reschedule with your school contact. Sessions cancelled outside your control are not held against you.
A girl refuses to participate or shuts down.
Stay calm, do not shame her, pick your battles, offer an alternative. A girl putting her head down is not an emergency. Follow up privately when tensions are low.
Do I have to use the fighter journals?
Mostly. Five weeks each season carry a set journal activity — in Season 1, weeks 3, 4, 6, 8 and 9 — and on those weeks it is not optional. The surveys are printed in the journal too, at weeks 1 and 10.
I keep running out of time. What can I cut?
Cut the review drill first, then extra mitt rounds. Never cut the burnout or the shout-outs — those are the two blocks she remembers.
Asked Most Often
Equipment and pay.
Equipment missing, or the space is not right for boxing.
You are responsible for the equipment issued to you and for returning it at season end. Report anything missing, damaged, or unsafe to Box United promptly. Never improvise around a safety risk.
How and when do I get paid? (1099 contract coaches)
A per-session rate set by the Coach Pay Table, paid as an independent contractor through Bill.com by direct deposit — typically a lump sum about two weeks after the season ends, based on verified attendance. Fall payday: Dec 1. Spring payday: May 1.
How does pay work for school-paid (MOU) coaches?
Box United does not pay you directly. You are compensated by your school or district through your existing employment. The MOU covers your role and responsibilities, not compensation.
Paid coaches are independent contractors, not employees. Box United does not withhold taxes or provide employee benefits.
When We Do Not Know The Answer
Let me confirm that for you.
Anything specific about an individual contract, a tax situation, or a child-safety scenario goes to Box United staff. A clear “let me confirm” protects you and the organisation. Never guess.
